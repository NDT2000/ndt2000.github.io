Personal Finance Tracker View code IN PROGRESS

May 2026 – Present

Can you trust your spending totals—and explore what comes next?
Finance Tracker brings manual entries and CSV imports into one ledger, with overlap review, corrections, and refunds that avoid double counting. Currency-aware dashboards explain recorded spending; saved what-if scenarios explore future cash, investments, and repayments.

Inside the engineering

Manual and imported records feed a single ledger so source rows are not counted twice. Durable import jobs preserve row outcomes and use bounded retries; ambiguous overlaps require review. Exact decimal arithmetic and separate currencies keep accounting precise. Planning scenarios stay separate from actual transactions, while a Python service supplies month-end spending forecasts.

The documented sample reconciles to USD 40 in net expenses and USD 100 in income, with EUR 7.50 kept separate. Re-importing the same files preserves the totals. Planning examples cover cash-reserve warnings and independently editable scenario copies. This is a local development project; saved bank accounts are labels, not live bank connections.
